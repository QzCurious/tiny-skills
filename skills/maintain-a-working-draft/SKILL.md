# Maintain a Working Draft

Use `Ground in Context` when the subject cannot be understood reliably from the current conversation and available Context Artifacts.

Identify the subject and the minimum Context Artifacts needed to recover its original intent, current state, material rationale and constraints, open questions, valid next steps, and possible terminal states. Recommend an Artifact Carrier suited to the user's access and available environment.

Use `Align Intent` to align with the user on the intended content and carrier before creating or updating an artifact.

Maintain the artifacts as the latest working state. Let the conversation retain the process history. Preserve in the artifacts any rationale, constraints, rejected alternatives, or prior events needed to interpret the current state. Preserve material uncertainty and distinguish tentative thinking from settled facts.

When the subject appears to have reached a terminal state, point this out and use `Align Intent` to determine whether to conclude, archive, supersede, or continue it.

The working draft is continuable when another conversation can recover the subject and a valid way to proceed without material distortion.
