name: resolve-state-authority
description: Resolve what authoritatively determines a target state, how it is derived and synchronized across material boundaries, and how strongly that synchronization can be trusted. Explore progressively to focus-relative closure while preserving a continuable partial resolution.
---

## Resolve the Target

Resolve authority per relevant claim or invariant rather than assuming the target's owner, storage, or immediate dependency is authoritative.

Start from the User's target at its current level of specificity. Refine it when evidence reveals materially different claims or state semantics, such as intended versus observed state.

Authority may resolve to one or more authoritative representations, or to a canonical derivation or decision rule over authoritative inputs.

## Trace Authority and Provenance

Trace authority-relevant relationships from the target, usually upstream through provenance and derivation.

Follow independent mutation, observation, feedback, side effects, or reconciliation when they can materially change the authority conclusion.

Verify semantics rather than labels such as cache, projection, replica, source, event, or callback.

## Resolve Synchronization

For each material derived representation, establish how it is maintained from authority.

Distinguish notifications, partial facts, and sufficient state-bearing inputs. Determine how missing state is re-sourced or recovered.

Assess relevant projection maintenance semantics: bootstrap, propagation, ordering and completeness, conflict or supersession, divergence detection, and recovery or reconciliation.

## Explore Progressively

Keep the Resolution Focus on the claims, concerns, and explicit exclusions that determine what must be resolved. Refine it from evidence without silently redirecting the User's investigation.

Pursue only frontiers that could materially change the current Resolution Focus.

Compress intermediate hops only after establishing that they do not materially affect authority, derivation, synchronization, responsibility, or failure semantics.

Prioritize relevant frontiers by conclusion impact and information value relative to exploration cost. Defer branches that remain relevant but are not currently worth pursuing.

## Keep the User Aligned

Surface material discoveries, why they matter, and the most useful next direction; omit routine investigation activity.

Continue autonomously through low-consequence exploration. Seek User steering when a choice materially changes scope, cost, interpretation, or outcome, and recommend a direction.

State provisional conclusions when useful, including what unresolved frontier could still change them.

## Reach Resolution

Resolution closes relative to the current Resolution Focus, not when every reachable dependency has been explored.

Stop when further exploration is unlikely to materially change the conclusion, an agreed horizon is reached, progress is blocked, or the User stops or redirects. Absence of further evidence is not proof of authority or resolution.

At every stop, preserve a continuable partial resolution: current focus, established conclusions, material unresolved or deferred frontiers, blockers, and the most useful next move.

Identify material projection weaknesses without redesigning them; recommend `improve-state-projection` when redesign is warranted.
