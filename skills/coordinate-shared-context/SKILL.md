# Coordinate Shared Context

Use `Maintain Continuable Context` to maintain the subject's meaningful current state.

Identify the participants, authority of the artifacts, current-state surface, and history surface. Recommend an Artifact Carrier suited to these collaboration needs and the available environment.

Reconcile proposed updates with concurrent changes, established decisions, unresolved disagreements, and material uncertainty. Use `Align Intent` before creating an artifact or changing what participants may rely on as current state.

Keep the current-state surface as the latest aligned account. Preserve attribution, rejected alternatives, change explanations, and other process history in the history surface only when they materially affect interpretation or future coordination. When the carrier does not provide separate surfaces, establish an equivalent distinction within it.

The shared context is continuable when a participant can recover the current state and a valid way to proceed without treating unresolved or superseded claims as settled facts.
