# Maintain Shared WIP

Use `Ground in Context` to inspect the existing Context Artifacts and relevant history before interpreting or changing the shared state.

Identify the subject, participants, authority of the artifact, current-state surface, and history surface. Recommend an Artifact Carrier suited to the collaboration needs and available environment.

Reconcile the proposed update with concurrent changes, established decisions, unresolved disagreements, and material uncertainty. Use `Align Intent` before creating an artifact or changing what participants may rely on as current state.

Maintain the current-state surface as the latest aligned account of the subject's intent, facts, decisions, material rationale and constraints, open questions, valid next steps, and possible terminal states. Preserve process, attribution, rejected alternatives, and change explanations in the history surface when they remain material to interpreting the current state. When the carrier does not provide separate surfaces, establish an equivalent distinction within it.

When the subject appears to have reached a terminal state, point this out and use `Align Intent` to determine whether to conclude, archive, supersede, or continue it.

The shared WIP is continuable when a participant can recover the current state and a valid way to proceed without treating unresolved or superseded claims as settled facts.
